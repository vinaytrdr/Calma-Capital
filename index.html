Calma Capital is a private investment firm built around disciplined research, selective opportunity, and long-term capital stewardship. The firm operates across U.S. index futures and metals, combining deep market structure analysis with a risk-first execution framework.

We operate at the intersection of institutional precision and private-client discretion. Every position begins with structure — not reaction, not sentiment, and never speculation for its own sake.

Our focus is narrow by design. NQ index futures and XAUUSD represent two of the deepest, most institutionally-driven markets in the world. Mastery of fewer instruments is the foundation of a durable edge.

Capital preservation precedes capital growth. That is not a limitation — it is the entire strategy.
